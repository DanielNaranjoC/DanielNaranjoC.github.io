Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Car Statics handles information when you use the mobile application. Car Statics is designed as an offline-first vehicle tracking app for vehicles, maintenance records, fuel fill-ups, statistics, preferences, and trip estimates.

Before publishing this policy, replace the contact placeholder in the Contact section with your real developer support email or support URL.

Information You Enter in the App

Car Statics lets you enter vehicle and car-care information, such as vehicle name, brand, model, year, plate, mileage or distance, maintenance records, maintenance types, fuel fill-ups, service provider, costs, notes, unit preferences, theme settings, and trip estimate inputs.

This information is stored locally on your device using Android local storage technologies. The app does not require an account, does not use Firebase, and does not send your vehicle, maintenance, fuel, cost, note, or preference data to a developer-operated server.

Advertising and Third-Party Data Processing

Car Statics uses Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner advertisements. When ads are shown, Google and its advertising partners may collect, receive, use, and share certain information from your device for purposes such as advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and service improvement.

According to Google's disclosures for the Google Mobile Ads SDK, data that may be collected and shared automatically can include IP address, user product interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID or app set ID. This data is handled by Google under Google's own policies and privacy controls.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices here:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can manage Google ad personalization settings here:
https://www.google.com/settings/ads

How Information Is Used

  • To save and display your vehicles, maintenance history, fuel history, and statistics.
  • To calculate consumption, costs, upcoming maintenance, and trip estimates.
  • To remember your app preferences on your device.
  • To display banner advertisements through Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Data Sharing

Car Statics does not sell your user-entered vehicle, maintenance, fuel, cost, note, or preference data. The app does not share that user-entered data with a developer-operated backend because no such backend is used.

Advertising-related data may be collected and shared by Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above. This is necessary to provide ads, measure ad performance, detect fraud, and support advertising services.

Data Storage and Security

User-entered app data is stored locally on your device. You are responsible for your device security, screen lock, backups, and operating system protections. Data transmitted by Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit according to Google's SDK disclosures.

Data Retention and Deletion

User-entered data remains on your device until you edit it, delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Because Car Statics does not create user accounts and does not operate a backend, there is no remote account deletion process for app data.

You may reset or delete your Android advertising ID through your Android device settings, subject to the options available on your device and Android version.

Children's Privacy

Car Statics is not intended for children under 13 years of age. The app is intended for general vehicle maintenance and fuel tracking use. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can review the issue.

International Users

Advertising services and related processing may involve servers or service providers located outside your country or region. Your use of the app may therefore involve processing of advertising-related data in other jurisdictions.
